About

I'm a researcher and applied scientist working at the intersection of statistical machine learning and large-scale production systems. My academic work spans survival analysis, federated learning, and algorithmic fairness — published across AAAI, KDD, SDM, and CIKM.

At Amazon's Central Seller Fulfillment team, I translate that research into shipping models: a Neural Causal Model that replaced a legacy DML pipeline and is now productionalized across 12 global marketplaces, an explainability framework engaging 15K+ sellers, and LLM-powered evaluation pipelines that compress evaluation cycles from weeks to hours.

Right now I'm building an evaluation framework for agentic AI, leading architecture improvements for the Seller Assistant agentic platform, and driving its multilingual international expansion. Recent work on grounded conversational recommendation — HTEF: Holistic Brand-Theme Alignment Scoring — won the Outstanding Paper Award at the GRAIL workshop.

Before Amazon I earned a Ph.D. in Information Systems at UMBC under Dr. Sanjay Purushotham, two master's degrees, and led the first Data Science section at the Central Bank of Bangladesh.

Fair Federated Survival Analysis

Md Mahmudur Rahman, Sanjay Purushotham

Defines new fairness criteria for time-to-event prediction and introduces the first federated survival framework that jointly optimizes accuracy and group fairness — without centralizing patient data.

Fairness Federated Survival

FedPseudo: Privacy-Preserving Pseudo Value-Based Deep Learning for Federated Survival Analysis

Md Mahmudur Rahman, Sanjay Purushotham

A pseudo-value reformulation that enables deep federated survival models with formal privacy guarantees, matching centralized accuracy on multi-institutional clinical cohorts.

Federated Competing Risk Analysis

Extends federated survival learning to the competing-risk setting, where multiple mutually exclusive events compete — a common case in oncology and cardiovascular outcomes.

Multi-state Survival Analysis using Pseudo value-based Deep Neural Networks

Md Mahmudur Rahman, Sanjay Purushotham
